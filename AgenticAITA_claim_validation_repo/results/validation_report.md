## Main methodological caveats

1. The binomial p≈0.34 claim is reproduced by a one-sided normal approximation. The exact one-sided binomial p-value is about 0.367 and the exact two-sided p-value is about 0.735.
2. The 2σ false-positive rate of 4.6% is the two-sided normal tail. The trigger is written as z_t ≥ 2.0, which is one-sided and would have a normal tail of about 2.28%. The return magnitude r_t is also non-negative, so a normal-tail interpretation needs more justification.
3. The transaction-cost sensitivity table is arithmetically consistent within cents-level rounding, but it is not a validation of live execution costs because the paper reports a dry-run without order placement.
4. Claims about live operation, zero human intervention, raw SQLite provenance, funding correction, and per-asset CBD effects require artefacts that are not included in the paper.
